The synergy and conflict between blockchain and artificial intelligence are complex and multifaceted. Blockchain technology can hinder economic competition in AI markets by introducing decentralization, transparency, and verifiability benefits that address concerns around black-box AI. The transparent record of transactions on blockchain can also enhance trustworthiness by documenting AI decision-making processes.

Blockchain's decentralized nature makes it a potential counterbalance to centralization in AI systems. However, this integration comes with significant challenges, including substantial computational resources required for training AI models and performing inference operations, storage constraints that increase costs and inefficiencies, and pseudo-anonymity and security issues arising in use cases where blockchain is used for decentralized AI training.

Blockchain can address limitations of AI by enabling fine-grained privacy configurations. However, conflicts between blockchain and AI operations pose significant challenges to integration.
